Executive Order 14233, Establishment of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and United States Digital Asset Stockpile, signed March 6, 2025, directs that bitcoin forfeited by the federal government be held in the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve rather than sold, with only narrow exceptions (such as returning assets to identifiable victims or use for law enforcement operations). This ended the long-standing practice of auctioning seized bitcoin, which is why no government bitcoin sales are recorded after March 2023.
